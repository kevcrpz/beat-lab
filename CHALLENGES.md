# Challenges

Your album, your sound. Start with the Day 1 steps, then pick from the menu.
There's no single right answer to any of these: make the songs your own.

- **Every change is a pull request**, reviewed and approved by a teammate
  before it's merged (README section 3).
- **Listen before you commit.** Copy your file from the GitHub editor, open
  your live link, and click **Test a draft**. Or use **✎ Edit** on the live
  link and **Copy as text** when it sounds right.

---

## Day 1: first steps (everyone)

1. **Get set up** (README section 1): the owner makes the team's copy, turns
   on the live link and invites everyone. Everyone accepts.
2. **Your first pull request.** Put your name on a song: change a `by:` line,
   or start your own song file (README section 2). Change one pattern while
   you're there, so you can hear your change on the live link. A teammate
   approves it and merges it.
3. **Write your team's plan.** One person opens **Issues** → **New issue**,
   titles it `Project plan`, and writes:
   - your album's name, and what it's about or what it should feel like
   - who's writing which song, and who's doing which job (README section 2)
   - which challenges below you're going to try
   - what you want to show at show and tell on **Tuesday, December 1**

## Make it yours

**Pick at least 3** (teams of 4: at least 4). Each one says whether it's for
one person or for the team. Write which ones you did in your `Project plan` issue.

### 🎵 A song of your own (each person)

Write a song from scratch in your own file. Start small: one beat that
loops, then add parts one at a time and listen after each one.

Hints: start with `kick` and `snare`, add a `bass` line that follows the
kick, then a `lead` or `pluck` tune on top. Most pop songs use the same few
sections over and over. Use **✎ Edit** → **Loop this section** to try ideas fast.

**Done when:** your song is on the album and it has at least **3 sections**,
at least **4 instruments** (at least one of them playing notes), and at least
one repeat in `play:` (like `chorus x2`).

### 🎤 Your own sound (one person, or everyone)

Record a sound with **Record a sound** on the live link (or on a phone):
a clap, a word, a pencil tap, a water bottle. Upload it to `sounds` and use it.

**Done when:** a song on the album uses your sound **twice**: once as a beat
(`x`), and once as a tune, with notes so it plays at different pitches
(like `C4 . E4 . G4 . C5 .`). The problems button says ✓ No problems.

### 🔁 Remix (two people)

Pick a teammate's song. Copy it into a new file, like
`songs/night-drive-remix.txt`, add it to `songs/list.txt`, and change it:
the tempo, at least two instruments, and the order of the sections. Keep
something that makes it recognizable.

**Done when:** both versions are on the album, and your pull request
description says what you kept, what you changed, and why it still sounds
like the same song.

### ⏱️ Tempo math (one person)

Make one song last **exactly one minute** (the site shows `1:00`) by
changing **only** its `tempo:`. Don't guess and check: work it out first.

Hint: one step lasts `60 ÷ tempo ÷ 4` seconds. Count how many sections your
`play:` line plays in total (`verse x2` counts as 2), and how many steps each one has.

**Done when:** the site shows `1:00` for that song, and your pull request
description shows your calculation.

### 🕺 Swing it (one person)

Add `swing: 0%`, then try `20%` and `40%` on the same song. Listen to the
hats closely. Keep the one you like best.

**Done when:** your song has a `swing:` line, and your pull request
description says what changed in the sound and which steps moved. (Hint:
the grid doesn't change, only the timing does.)

### 💿 The album (team)

Make it a real album, not a pile of songs:

- `album.txt`: a title, your team name, a theme that fits, and at least two
  `about:` paragraphs of liner notes (who made what, and what each song is about)
- `songs/list.txt` in the order that sounds best from start to finish
- every song has a title and a `by:`
- the two sample songs are gone from the track list (keep the cowbell if you like it)
- *optional:* your own cover picture: upload it to `images` and add
  `cover: images/your-picture.png`

**Done when:** all of that is true on your live link, with ✓ No problems.

### 🔀 Conflict on purpose (two people, 10 minutes)

Merge conflicts happen when two people change the same lines. Make one
happen, then fix it, so it isn't scary when it happens for real.

1. Two people each start a pull request that adds a different line to the
   **bottom** of `songs/list.txt`.
2. Merge the first one.
3. The second one now says *This branch has conflicts*. Resolve it so both
   lines stay (README section 6).

**Done when:** both pull requests are merged and both songs are on the album.

### 🔢 Sound is numbers (one person)

Download two songs as WAV files: one about half as long as the other.
Compare the file sizes.

**Done when:** you've added a comment to your `Project plan` issue that
explains the sizes using these facts: the file stores 44,100 numbers
(samples) per second, for each of 2 speakers, and each number takes 2 bytes.
How many megabytes would a 3-minute song be?

## Before December 1: show and tell

Very short and informal: two minutes, just show what you made.

- [ ] Your live link is open and loaded, with **✓ No problems**.
- [ ] You've checked that sound works on the classroom speakers.
- [ ] You've picked 20–30 seconds of one song to play. (Tip: add
      `?track=2` to the address to open the second song directly.)
- [ ] Each person can point to one thing they made.
- [ ] Your team can name one thing that surprised you, or that was harder
      than it looked.
